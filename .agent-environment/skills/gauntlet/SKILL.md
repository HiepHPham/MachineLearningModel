# Gauntlet

Improve the system until its acceptance criteria are demonstrated. Follow the
standing guidance for scope, routing, ownership, and authoring. Keep a compact
repository artifact describing the intended outcome, current design, evidence,
open findings, and next work so another session can continue.

For a continuation, read the existing artifacts and verify that their assumptions
still fit current evidence and the user's intent. For new work, establish the
product needs and useful success criteria before choosing the implementation.

## Challenge the foundations

Derive plausible solutions from primary needs and data, then compare them with the
current design. Independent derivation or blinded comparison is useful when an
incumbent could bias judgment. Test whether data and fixtures represent actual
scale, shape, and failure cases. Examine whether each surface serves a user need,
and whether boundaries force one layer to compensate for another.

Integrate the evidence into the current design and open questions. Choose changes
by demonstrated value, migration cost, and reversibility. Use evidence to revisit
any previous choice.

## Iterate

Give workers bounded partitions and independent worktrees. Start reviews as
completed partitions become available; review integrated seams after combining
them. Objective checks establish data and runtime facts before subjective review.
Bind evidence to the exact candidate and representative state being judged.

Use independent reviewers with the context appropriate to their job. A user-view
review benefits from seeing the rendered result without the author's rationale;
an invariant review needs the specification and implementation. Route actionable
findings by their underlying cause. Confirm suspected defects before assigning
fixes and keep unresolved claims visible with their evidence.

When repeated tuning fails, reconsider the underlying design and verification
method. Refresh the foundation assessment when new evidence changes the problem.
Retain objective evidence as needed; maintain the work artifact around current
design and open work.

Finish when the agreed criteria and material findings are resolved with evidence,
or report the specific remaining prerequisite. Carry authorized release through
the shared ship workflow.
